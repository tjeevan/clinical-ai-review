Are You Still Human?

Three true stories, ten patterns, and one quiet choice.

View Kindle edition on Amazon ↗

Kindle Edition · English · 37 pages · Published 27 May 2026

T. Jeevaraj · Author

Three true scenes from one Sri Lankan doctor's notebook.

A software engineer in Colombo turns a chance meeting with an old friend into a scheduling calculation.

A father drives his fifteen-year-old son home at one in the morning, and the son says eight words: “Appa, you are prompting me to get your desired answer.”

A senior accountant, six months after her mother's death, cannot find her grief anywhere. “My brain pre-empts the grief. It decided grief was inefficient. And now I cannot find it.”

After years of refining prompts, optimising workflows, and managing systems for ten hours a day, we have quietly begun managing the people in our houses the same way. The friend at the gate. The child in the passenger seat. The grief that should have arrived and didn't.

This isn't a book against AI. The author uses these tools every day and is in postgraduate training in health informatics. It is a short book about the small, almost invisible ways our work habits have followed us home.

Three stories. Ten patterns. One quiet choice.
