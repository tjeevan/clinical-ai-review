# Clinical AI Review

An independent personal educational initiative exploring AI-generated medical information through clinical reasoning, scientific evidence and critical appraisal.

Website: https://drjeevaraj.com

Reviews examine individual AI responses, not entire models. The proposed editorial rubric is not a validated clinical instrument. This website does not provide individual medical advice, certification or paid reviews. It is separate from postgraduate research and institutional duties; no institutional endorsement is implied.

General educational suggestions and corrections may be sent through the academic correspondence link on the About page. Do not send personal clinical information or confidential material.

Google Analytics is inactive in this release pending privacy and consent safeguards.
