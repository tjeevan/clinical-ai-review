# Clinical AI Review

**Independent Educational Publication**

**Website:** https://drjeevaraj.com

Clinical AI Review is an independent educational website dedicated to the critical examination of AI-generated medical information.

The publication explores the clinical accuracy, scientific evidence, completeness, clarity, and potential patient-safety implications of medical information produced by artificial intelligence tools.

Its purpose is to encourage careful interpretation of AI-generated medical content through clinical reasoning and evidence-based assessment.

## What We Review

- **Clinical accuracy** — Whether medical information is factually correct and clinically appropriate.
- **Scientific evidence** — Whether claims are supported by relevant and reliable medical literature.
- **Patient safety** — Potentially misleading advice, omissions, contraindications, and clinical risks.
- **Completeness and clarity** — Whether important clinical context and uncertainty are adequately communicated.
- **Opportunities for improvement** — How medical explanations could be made more accurate, balanced, and useful.

## Review Approach

Each published assessment aims to document the original AI-generated response, relevant clinical claims, supporting evidence, identified limitations, and suggested improvements.

Reviews concern specific AI-generated content. They do not constitute certification or comprehensive validation of an AI model.
