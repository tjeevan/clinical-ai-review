Methodology / Version 1.1 · November 2026

How we review medical information

Scientific evidence, clinical experience and human judgment.

An independent educational approach to understanding AI-generated medical answers, not evaluating, ranking or certifying AI models.

STEP 01

Document the original question and answers

Each article begins with a medical question or hypothetical clinical scenario. The original question and complete written AI answers are presented wherever possible. Source details, including platform, model version and date, are retained when available. Platform identities may be omitted from publication. Any omissions, redactions or substantive changes are disclosed. Identifiable patient information is not used.

STEP 02

Understand the medical information

The answers are considered in relation to the original question, intended audience and clinical circumstances. Attention is given to medical explanations, diagnostic possibilities, recommended actions, uncertainty and practical guidance. Useful aspects are acknowledged alongside areas that may benefit from clarification.

STEP 03

Review the scientific evidence

Medical information is considered alongside relevant scientific literature, clinical guidelines and established medical knowledge. National and international guidelines, systematic reviews and original research are consulted where appropriate. References are checked for existence, relevance and support for the specific claim. Currency, applicability and differences between healthcare settings are considered.

STEP 04

Present a human clinical perspective

Each article includes a personal clinical perspective informed by the author’s medical knowledge, experience and local healthcare context. This is distinguished from established scientific evidence. Clinical judgment may vary with patient circumstances, experience, resources and local guidelines. The author’s opinion is one professional perspective, not the only possible approach.

STEP 05

Offer constructive suggestions

Suggestions aim to improve the usefulness, clarity, clinical relevance and safety of AI-generated medical information. The intention is not to find faults with AI, but to explore how human clinical experience can complement it. Important clinical concerns are explained with appropriate justification and supporting evidence.

STEP 06

Acknowledge limitations

Each article discusses specific answers at a particular point in time. Responses may vary with prompt wording, model version, geography, personalisation and access to external information. These articles are not formal research, validated clinical assessments or systematic comparisons of AI models. Where AI assists with literature exploration, drafting or presentation, the author remains responsible for reviewing and approving the published clinical interpretation.

Key areas of clinical consideration

  • Clinical accuracy
  • Scientific evidence
  • Patient safety
  • Completeness of information
  • Clarity of communication
  • Clinical context and practical guidance

Our approach

Artificial intelligence can provide valuable medical information. Human clinical experience can contribute additional context, interpretation and practical judgment. Clinical AI Review explores how these two perspectives can complement each other.

This publication is intended for general education. It does not provide individual medical advice or represent the views of any employing institution, postgraduate training programme or professional organisation.
