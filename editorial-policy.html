Editorial policy / November 2026

Independence and responsibility.

The standards and limits guiding this educational publication.

Independent perspective

Content reflects the author's personal educational analysis and does not represent any employer, training institution, government agency, AI developer or professional body. No institutional endorsement is claimed.

Scope

Published reviews concern selected AI-generated medical information, not direct clinical care. Findings are contextual assessments of particular outputs, not certifications or comprehensive validation of AI models.

Evidence and conflicts

Substantive findings should cite relevant sources and explain uncertainty. Material conflicts of interest relevant to an individual review will be disclosed. Reviews will not be presented as independent if a material relationship compromises that description.

Corrections and updates

Substantive errors will be corrected with a visible date and change note on the relevant article. Older assessments may be revised or marked as superseded when evidence or model behavior changes. No public correction log is claimed until actual reviews are published.

Privacy and screenshots

Do not submit patient records, identifiable health information or confidential institutional documents. AI output screenshots must preserve the relevant context and remove personal identifiers. This static site does not provide secure patient-data submission or clinical consultation.

Website data

This site does not currently include a visitor login, contact form, analytics script or advertising tracker. GitHub Pages hosting may process technical access information under GitHub's own policies. External links, including Amazon, are governed by their respective sites.

Book

The separate book page links to a literary work by the author. It is not evidence of clinical AI evaluation quality or an endorsement of a commercial AI product.
